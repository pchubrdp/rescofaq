Support & Transparency

How Resco Works

No hidden terms, no vague promises. Just honest answers about how your phone earns you money while you sleep.

🤔 How does Resco actually make money to pay me?

Unlike scam apps that promise free money out of nowhere, we run a transparent model. When you keep Resco active, we use a tiny, unnoticeable fraction of your unused internet bandwidth to help businesses verify web data (like checking if ads are displaying correctly). We get paid by these companies, and we pass the majority of that revenue directly to you.

🔒 Is my personal data safe? Do you read my files?

100% Yes. Resco operates completely in a sandbox. We do not ask for storage permissions, we cannot see your photos, and we do not monitor your personal messages or browsing history. We strictly utilize isolated network data.

💸 What are the withdrawal options and limits?

We believe in letting you choose how you get paid. Once you hit the minimum threshold, you can instantly withdraw your rewards to:

  • Direct UPI Transfer (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm)
  • Google Play Gift Cards
  • Amazon Pay Balances

⏳ What is the exact payment timeline?

No "7 to 14 business days" nonsense. UPI withdrawals and Gift card codes are processed by our automated system and typically arrive in your account within 2 to 12 hours of your request.

🤝 How does the Refer & Earn policy work?

When you invite a friend, you earn a 10% lifetime commission on everything they earn—without reducing their balance! It’s a win-win. Just share your unique invite code from the app dashboard.

🎥 Where can I see live payment proofs?

We don't hide behind stock images. Check out our official YouTube channel and Instagram page where we post daily tutorials, live UPI withdrawal proofs, and updates straight from the developer.
